TITLE: “Serious Concerns About the Authenticity of Bark Leads”

REVIEW:
I created an account on Bark because I was interested in using the platform to find customers for my services. However, before purchasing any credits, I decided to carefully examine how the platform actually works. After monitoring the platform for a while, I have reached the conclusion that I simply do not have enough confidence in what is being presented to me to spend any money on it. The app consistently shows me around 178 “matching leads”, yet when I check the map and try to see the actual jobs available in my area, I can only see around 15. So my question is very simple: where are the rest of the 178 leads? Furthermore, I have noticed that many of the same requests appear day after day. From the perspective of a business being asked to purchase credits simply to access contact details, this raises some serious questions. Bark does not guarantee me a customer or even a job. Essentially, I am being asked to pay for access to someone’s contact details, after which I still have to contact that person and find out whether they still need the service, whether they have already found someone else, whether they are still interested, and whether they will even respond to me. So what exactly am I paying for? Based on what I have observed, I have even started to question whether all of these leads genuinely represent real people who have actually requested these services. I am not claiming that Bark creates fake leads, but what I am seeing on the platform gives me reason to have this concern, and I would like a clear explanation from you. I would like to know how you verify that each lead comes from a real person, how you verify that the request is still active, and why the number of “matching leads” displayed is so different from what I can actually see on the map. This is precisely why I have not purchased any credits, and I do not intend to purchase any until Bark can provide a transparent explanation and give me confidence that the leads you are charging businesses to access genuinely come from real people who are actively looking for those services.

After years as a customer, I expected a proper explanation, evidence and a prompt resolution—not an unsupported allegation followed by a generic response. Based on the high cost of leads, limited financial protection and the way my account was handled, I cannot recommend Bark to other small businesses.

TITLE: Works out expensive

REVIEW:
I love the concept of Bark but in reality it ends up being very expensive. One of the really frustrating aspects for me is the fact that it costs you credits even if you don’t end up speaking to the customer. 90% of the customer that I contact don’t answer their phones even when I phone immediately after them posting the advert or don’t respond to the messages I send them. Why post an advert if you aren’t going to respond? This is the major flaw with the concept. It would be good if you could give a review to customers so that prospective professionals could see this before deciding whether to by the lead or not but I guess Bark wouldn’t make as much money this way. I much prefer using Bidvine because if the customer doesn’t end up reading your request, you get the credit back.
